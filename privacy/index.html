Privacy

This notice explains how the public website handles information submitted through it.

Information submitted through the contact form

When you use the contact form, the site collects the name, email address, and message you choose to provide. That information is used to review and respond to the business inquiry. The form also includes practical anti-abuse controls, such as a hidden honeypot field, timing checks, and submission throttling. Please do not include confidential, sensitive, or regulated information in an initial website message.

Service providers and analytics

The contact form uses EmailJS to transmit a submitted inquiry. The site also uses Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate site use, such as page visits and interactions with public resources. These providers may process limited technical information, including browser, device, IP-derived, and event data, according to their own policies. The site does not use website analytics to make automated decisions about individuals.

How information is used and shared

Information from an inquiry is used for communication about the request and related professional services. It is not sold. It is not published on this site. Information may be processed by the service providers described above when they are needed to operate the form, secure the site, or measure site performance. Disclosure may also occur when required to meet a legal obligation or protect the site and its users.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the contact form and can control or clear browser cookies and local storage through your browser settings. To ask a question about a prior inquiry or this notice, use the contact form and include enough context for the request to be understood. This public notice may be updated when the site or its service providers change.
